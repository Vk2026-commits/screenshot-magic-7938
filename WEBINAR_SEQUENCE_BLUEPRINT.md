# Build Your First AI Income Stream — Weekly Webinar Email Blueprint

**Model used:** The supplied AI Business Summit sequence.
**Adaptation principle:** Keep its warm, plain-text, anticipation-building style, but right-size the frequency for a **single weekly 7:00 PM Central webinar** rather than a multi-day summit.

## What the reference sequence does well

The examples use a clear progression:

1. **Welcome and inbox training** — confirms registration, establishes the sender address, explains what messages are coming, and asks for a reply to improve inbox placement.
2. **Value and preparation** — explains what the event will help the registrant accomplish and gives a simple assignment before the event.
3. **Participation / readiness** — invites an action that increases commitment, such as joining a community or completing a technical check.
4. **Countdown and urgency** — shifts from broad value to exact timing and access instructions as the event gets closer.
5. **Live-now access** — a short message with one job: get people into the event.

The style is effective because it feels like a personal note, uses short paragraphs, gives each email **one job**, and asks the recipient to take small actions before the event.

## Recommended weekly cadence

| Email | Timing | Goal | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Welcome / confirmation | Immediately after registration | Confirm the seat, establish the sender, train the inbox | Reply `I’M IN` or add to contacts |
| 2. Choose your focus | Wednesday, 10:00 AM Central | Build anticipation and get the attendee to choose one outcome | Reply or write down the one problem they want AI to help solve |
| 3. Tomorrow night | Saturday, 7:00 PM Central | Turn a passive registration into a plan to attend | Add to calendar / save the Zoom email |
| 4. Today at 7 PM | Sunday, 10:00 AM Central | Re-state the promise and prepare for the live session | Decide the one income path or business problem to bring |
| 5. Doors open soon | Sunday, 6:00 PM Central | Remove friction and prompt early attendance | Open the Zoom link; join 5–10 minutes early |
| 6. Starting now | Sunday, 7:00 PM Central | Get registrants into the live room | Join Zoom now |
| 7. Follow-up | Monday morning | Move attendees and no-shows to the next step | Watch replay, register for next Sunday, or take the primary offer |

> **Important:** Do not send all seven messages to someone who registered shortly before the webinar. The automation should only send messages whose scheduled time occurs **after** that person registered.

## What should not be copied directly

| Reference tactic | Why not copy it exactly | Better webinar version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A full week of daily summit content | A multi-day summit has more moving parts and several sessions; a weekly 60-minute webinar does not need the same volume. | One value/preparation message, two Sunday reminders, and one live-now message. |
| Community-group invitation | Useful only if the community will be moderated and creates real value. An empty group hurts trust. | Add only if there is an active community or a structured pre-event prompt. |
| Tech-check call | Valuable for a dashboard-based multi-day event; unnecessary for a standard Zoom webinar. | Use a calendar link and tell attendees to join 5–10 minutes early. |
| Upgrade pitch before the event | Works when there is a clear premium tier; can distract from first-time attendance when no offer is defined. | Keep the pre-webinar focus on show-up. Place the main offer after the session. |

## Required automation rules

1. **Assign each registrant to the next Sunday**, which is already handled by the database.
2. **Only send future scheduled messages**—never send a missed Wednesday or Saturday email to someone who registers Sunday afternoon.
3. **Record every reminder send** in a private ledger so retries cannot duplicate messages.
4. **Stop all pre-event reminders after the webinar starts.**
5. **Segment the Monday follow-up** into attendee and no-show tracks when Zoom attendance data is available. Until then, use a single follow-up with replay/next-Sunday options.
6. **Use a recurring Zoom meeting** or update the protected join secret before each unique weekly meeting.
7. **Include unsubscribe and mailing-address footer** on marketing/reminder emails. The immediate registration confirmation can remain transactional, but the sequence should follow commercial-email requirements.

## Inputs still needed for the final automated version

- The business mailing address for the reminder footer
- Privacy-policy URL
- Unsubscribe/preference-center URL or provider workflow
- Whether there will be a replay, its URL, and its expiration policy
- The primary Monday CTA: strategy call, program, service, offer, or next webinar
- Whether reply-based engagement should be monitored
- Whether Ricky wants to invite people to a real community before the webinar
